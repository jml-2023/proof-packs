## What this can't show

- A trend: it is one day. Compare only with a count on the same boards, in the same month of the year.
- Hires: one posting can hire several people, and one role can be posted in several cities.
- Roles that never reach a public board, such as referrals and internships filled directly.
- Cause: a count of postings can't tell you why the bottom rung is the size it is.
